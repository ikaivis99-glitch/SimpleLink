Privacy Policy — SimpleLink

This policy explains what the Discord application SimpleLink, operated by RKid for the EnjoyRust community, stores and why.

Short version: SimpleLink connects your Discord account to your Steam account so we can give you your Verified role and your in-game rewards. We store that link and the small amount of data that comes with it. We do not sell anything to anyone, and you can remove your link yourself at any time.

1. What SimpleLink does

You open our website, log in with Discord and then with Steam, and we save that the two accounts belong to the same person. Your Discord account is then given the "Verified" role on our server, and rewards on our Rust game servers are matched to your Steam account.

2. What we store

We do not store your email address, your real name, your payment details, your Discord messages or any chat content.

3. Why we are allowed to store it

We process this data to provide a service you asked for when you chose to link your accounts, and because we have a legitimate interest in keeping our servers and rewards free of abuse.

4. Who else sees it

Your data is not sold, rented or shared for advertising. It is shared only with:

  • Discord — because the bot runs on their platform and your roles are given through Discord;
  • Hetzner Online GmbH — the hosting provider where the website and the bot run;
  • authorities, if we are legally required to.

Our staff can look up which Steam account belongs to a Discord account, and the other way round, so they can help players and deal with rule breaking.

5. Security

Data is stored on access-controlled servers. Only RKid and EnjoyRust staff members can reach it. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect it.

6. Removing your data

  • You can remove your own link at any time on our website: simplelink.enjoyrust.com/unlink.php. Your Verified role is removed with it. When you do this, we keep a record of the removed link, with the same data, in a separate table so staff can see repeated abuse. Write to us and we delete that record too.
  • If you leave our Discord server, your link is deleted automatically.
  • You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete all of it. Write to kay.non@yahoo.com and we will respond within 7 days.

7. Children

The bot is not intended for anyone below Discord's minimum age for their country. If we learn that we hold data belonging to such a user, we delete it.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Significant changes will be announced in our Discord server.
