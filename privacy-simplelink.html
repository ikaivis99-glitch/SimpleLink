Privacy Policy — SimpleLink

This policy explains what the Discord application SimpleLink, operated by RKid, stores and why.

Short version: we store only what the bot needs to work, we do not sell anything to anyone, and you can ask us to delete your data at any time.

1. What we store

DataWhyKept for
Discord user IDTo know who ran a command and apply your settingsIndefinitely
Server (guild) ID and channel IDsTo store per-server settings and know where to replyUntil the bot is removed from the server
Settings you configureTo keep your preferences between usesUntil you change or delete them
Command usage recordsTo diagnose errors and prevent abuseIndefinitely

We do not collect your email address, real name, IP address, payment details or private messages that the bot has not been directly asked to process.

2. Why we are allowed to store it

We process this data to provide a service you asked for, and because we have a legitimate interest in keeping the bot working and free of abuse.

3. Who else sees it

Your data is not sold, rented, or shared for advertising. It is shared only with:

  • Discord — because the bot runs on their platform;
  • Hetzner Online GmbH — the hosting provider where the bot runs;
  • authorities, if we are legally required to.

4. Security

Data is stored on access-controlled servers. Only RKid and Enjoy Team Members can reach it. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect it.

5. Your rights

You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Write to kay.non@yahoo.com and we will respond within 7 days. Removing the bot from a server also deletes that server's settings.

6. Children

The bot is not intended for anyone below Discord's minimum age for their country. If we learn that we hold data belonging to such a user, we delete it.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Significant changes will be announced in our support server.
