Privacy Policy for Solo Monthly

Last Updated: September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Solo Monthly ("the Bot", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use or interact with the Bot.

By using Solo Monthly, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. About Solo Monthly

Solo Monthly is a Discord application designed to display the current online player count of our connected game server directly on its Discord profile.

The Bot operates automatically and does not provide user-facing commands. Its primary function is to retrieve the current player count from our game server and display information such as:

300/350 Players

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how the Bot is configured and used, Solo Monthly may collect and store the following information:

Discord Information

  • Discord User ID
  • Discord server/guild ID
  • Discord role information
  • Information required to identify users and apply server-specific permissions or settings

The Bot does not intentionally collect or store Discord message content.

Game Server Information

The Bot may collect information from our own game server, including:

  • Current online player count
  • Maximum server player capacity
  • Server status and related operational information

Certain game server information may be stored on our own infrastructure for operational, monitoring, and historical purposes.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information only when necessary to operate and maintain enjoybot.

  • Displaying the current game server player count
  • Identifying Discord users when required for configuration or permissions
  • Reading Discord roles for server-specific settings
  • Maintaining server configurations
  • Monitoring and maintaining the reliability of the Bot
  • Troubleshooting technical issues
  • Maintaining historical game server information where applicable

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

4. Message Content

Solo Monthly does not require or use Discord message content for its core functionality.

The Bot does not intentionally read, process, or store the content of messages sent by Discord users.

5. Data Storage

Information collected by Solo Monthly may be stored on servers operated or controlled by us.

We may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Bot's functionality, maintain server configurations, troubleshoot issues, and maintain operational or historical records.

The retention period may vary depending on the type and purpose of the information.

6. Data Sharing
